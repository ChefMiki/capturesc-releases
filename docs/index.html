Screenshots
without the wait

Press Print Screen, select an area or click a window, mark it up right there and paste it anywhere. CaptureSC replaces the Windows Snipping Tool with something faster and calmer.

Download for Windows 11

Free preview. About 92 MB, nothing else to install.

Everything happens where you capture

  • Ready instantly

    The screen freezes in about a tenth of a second, with a magnifier for pixel-exact edges.

  • Click a window

    No dragging needed. Or select any area and adjust it with handles and arrow keys.

  • Mark it up in place

    Arrows, rectangles, ellipses, highlight, text and numbered steps, right on the selection.

  • Redact for good

    Pixels are destroyed, not blurred, so emails and phone numbers cannot be recovered.

  • Saved and copied

    The moment you let go, it is in your Screenshots folder and on the clipboard.

  • Pin, history, your keys

    Keep a capture floating on top, find old ones, and choose your own shortcuts.

Install in a minute

  1. Unzip the downloadAnywhere you like, for example your Downloads folder.
  2. Double-click Install.cmdClick Yes when Windows asks. The installer adds the preview certificate, installs CaptureSC and starts it.
  3. Press Print ScreenThat is it. CaptureSC waits in the tray next to the clock.
About Smart App Control This preview is not code-signed yet. If Smart App Control is on, Windows may block CaptureSC. The installer checks for you and explains how to turn it off in Windows Security, under App & browser control.

Private by design

Everything stays on your computer. No account, no analytics, and nothing is ever uploaded. Your screenshots are plain files in your Pictures folder.
